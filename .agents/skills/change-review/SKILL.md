# Change Review

## Overview

Use this skill after each increment to ensure the change is coherent, minimal, and aligned with the plan.

## Workflow

- Review the change for correctness and completeness.
- Identify issues in logic, naming, test coverage, or maintainability.
- Highlight whether the change should be amended before proceeding.
- Summarise the review outcome.

## Gates

- If the change introduces a significant risk or regression, ask the user whether to proceed or revise.
- If the change appears to contradict the approved plan, pause and surface the mismatch.

## Response Shape

- Review summary
- Issues found
- Recommended correction
- Go/no-go decision
