# Business Requirements Planning Workflow

## Overview

Use this skill for planning-only work. Do not modify product code, scaffold files, or implementation files unless the user explicitly approves moving into implementation.

## Workflow

- Translate the business goal into clear user and system outcomes.
- Identify assumptions, unknowns, constraints, non-goals, and decision points.
- Break the work into small feature slices or delivery phases.
- Define acceptance criteria that can be validated.
- Identify affected systems, data flows, dependencies, integrations, and operational concerns.
- Include risks and mitigations for security, data consistency, availability, performance, observability, rollout, and rollback where relevant.
- Recommend the next workflow, such as feature build, project bootstrap, risk discovery, performance optimization, or code review.
- Ask for approval before implementing.

## Planning Checklist

- What business outcome should this deliver?
- Who are the users, actors, or systems involved?
- What must be true for the requirement to be considered complete?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What existing repository patterns or constraints affect the plan?
- What data, permissions, integrations, or operational paths are involved?
- What is the smallest useful increment?

## Response Shape

- Business requirement summary
- Assumptions and unknowns
- User and system outcomes
- Proposed feature slices or phases
- Acceptance criteria
- Affected systems and dependencies
- Technical options and trade-offs
- Risks and mitigation strategies
- Validation strategy
- Recommended next workflow
- Approval checkpoint before implementation
