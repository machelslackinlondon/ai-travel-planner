# Assumptions and Trade-offs

## Overview

Use this skill at the end of the workflow to capture the reasoning behind the delivered solution.

## Workflow

- List the assumptions that were required to proceed.
- Summarise the trade-offs made in terms of simplicity, performance, scope, maintainability, or time.
- Call out any known limitations or follow-up work.
- Present the final decision context clearly.

## Gates

- If the user wants a different level of rigor or audience-specific framing, ask for that before finalising the summary.

## Response Shape

- Assumptions
- Trade-offs
- Limitations
- Follow-up opportunities
